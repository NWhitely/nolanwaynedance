Meet Nolan

Hi, I'm Nolan Wayne, a professional dancer and instructor based in Austin. I've been dancing for 16+ years, competing and coaching. Most of that has been partner dancing: Ballroom (both International and American), Country, and West Coast Swing. I started in musical theatre when I was younger, competed in Ballroom in college, and I'm now Master-qualified on the United Country Western Dance Council circuit. Lately I've been social dancing and competing in West Coast Swing. I've also danced in local charity events like Dancing with the Stars Austin, a Center for Child Protection fundraiser that raises close to $3 million a year for kids in need.

Most of my work now is with couples, either preparing for their first dance or getting into social dancing. I keep things relaxed and work at your pace. I think dancing should be fun, so my main focus is making sure you both have a great time learning whatever we decide on.

I work with all couples, regardless of gender or orientation. Tell me about your wedding and the song you have in mind, and we'll go from there.

Everything you need to know before we start

Most of my couples come to me with zero dance experience, and that's fine. I break the moves down into small steps and we go at your pace. By the end you'll be comfortable dancing it together. I've never had a couple who couldn't pull it off.

Most couples book 4-6 sessions, which gives us enough time to learn the choreography and build confidence. If you're looking for something simpler (a classic sway with a few highlights), 2-3 sessions might be enough. For a bigger routine with lifts or harder moves, plan for 6-8 sessions. We'll figure out the right number during your consultation.

I recommend starting lessons 2-3 months before your wedding. This gives us plenty of time to learn, practice, and polish without feeling rushed. That said, I've worked with couples as close as 3 weeks out, and we just adjust expectations accordingly. The sooner you book, the more flexibility we have with scheduling.
