Questions

We have two left feet. Can you really teach us to dance?
Yes. Most of my couples start with zero experience. I break every move into small steps and build from there. I've never had a couple who couldn't do it.
How many lessons do we need?
Most couples book 4 to 6. A simple sway with a few highlights takes 2 to 3. A bigger routine with lifts or harder moves takes 6 to 8. We set the number at the consultation.
How far in advance should we book?
Start lessons 2 to 3 months out. That covers learning, practice and polish. I've worked with couples 3 weeks out; the routine scales to the time. Book early for the best lesson times.
Can you work with any song?
Almost any. The tempo and structure of the song decide the style, and I match the dance to it. I've built routines to everything from Ed Sheeran to classic country. No song yet? I'll help you pick one that works.
What if one of us is more experienced than the other?
Common. I build the routine around both of you. The stronger dancer gets more to do, the other gets moves they can execute with confidence. On the floor, the gap won't show.
Do you offer lessons for parent dances too?
Yes. Single sessions or short packages. Keep it simple, or add a surprise.
