Questions

I've truly never danced. Is that okay?
More than okay. Most of my students start with zero experience. I break every move down and we go at your pace. You don't need rhythm or any background to start.
Do I need a partner?
No. I teach plenty of solo students, and you can learn to lead or follow without bringing anyone. If you have a partner you want to learn with, bring them.
Which style should I start with?
Whichever one you like the sound of. Country two-step is an easy start here in Texas. Ballroom if you want something classic. West Coast Swing if you want something for social nights. Not sure? Tell me what music you listen to and I'll suggest one.
I already dance socially. Can you help me level up?
Yes, especially country-western, where I competed at the top levels. I can clean up your technique, add to your moves, and work on styling. Same for West Coast Swing and Ballroom at an intermediate level.
How many lessons will I need?
It depends on the goal. A few lessons cover the basics of a style. Weekly lessons are for steady progress. There's no required package.
Where do lessons happen?
Your choice: in your home (I come to you), at a partner studio in Austin with professional floors and mirrors, or over video if you're not local.
