# Facebook Marketplace listing — Wedding dance (low-flag version)

Goal: read like a normal person posting a real service, not an ad. No graphics,
no website, no @handle, no "message me for details," no $0 price. Those are the
things that pass the first scan and get pulled 24-48hrs later.

---

## Category
Services → look for "Lessons" / "Classes" or "Other" if there's no dance fit.
Set the location to Austin.

## Title
```
Wedding First Dance Lessons in Austin TX
```
(plain and literal beats clever here)

## Price
```
90
```
List the real per-lesson rate, not "Free." A real number reads as a genuine
listing. You can still offer the no-pressure first lesson in the description —
just don't put $0 in the price field, that's a spam flag on a service.

## Description
```
I teach wedding couples their first dance. Most people I work with have never
danced before and feel a little weird about it. That's normal, it's most of who
I teach.

We start with your song and find something that fits the two of you. Could be a
slow dance, a country two-step, a bit of swing. Depends on the song and how far
you want to take it. Doesn't have to be big choreography unless you want that.

How it works:
- 45 minute lessons, $90 each
- In your home, at a studio, or over video
- Austin and the surrounding area
- 16+ years dancing, teaching and competing

The first lesson is low key. We run through your song and see what's doable, no
commitment after that.

Send me your song and your wedding date and I'll tell you what it'd take to be
ready in time.
```

## Photos — this is the part that matters most
Post REAL photos, not the branded cards. The cards have a URL, a handle, and a
"Book a FREE lesson" button on them, which is exactly what the second-pass
review removes (it reads as an off-platform ad).

Order matters. Lead with a real photo; the branded card goes second.
- **Photo 1 (lead):** `assets/hollingsworth-2.jpg` — a real couple's first dance
  at a venue, phone-shot, looks like someone actually took it. This is what passes
  review and what people see first.
- **Photo 2:** `marketplace-wedding-safe.jpg` — a stripped-down branded card (name,
  service, Austin, styles, credential) with the URL, @handle, and "Book" button
  removed, so it carries your identity without tripping the off-platform-ad filter.
  Built by `make_marketplace_safe_card.py`. Do NOT lead with this one.
- **Photo 3 (optional):** `assets/hollingsworth-1.jpg` (same event).

Never post the full promo card (`promo-wedding.jpg`) here — that's the one with
the URL/handle/button that gets pulled.

Avoid `photo1`, `photo5`, `photo7` here — they're the polished black-and-white
editorial shots that read as stock, which is its own smaller flag.

Other good options if you want to shoot fresh:
- You dancing with a couple (or any two people) in a normal room or studio
- A lesson in progress, phone-shot, slightly candid
- A couple dancing at a real reception
- The studio floor / space

No text on the image. No logo. No website. No CTA button. If you want one
branded card, put it LAST, never first. The first photo should look like a
photo someone took, not a flyer.

## Posting habits that keep you up
- Don't repost the identical listing over and over — renew/edit the existing one
- Vary the wording a little if you do repost
- Keep all contact inside Facebook messages (don't push people to the site or a
  phone number in the listing — do that after they message you)

## Where the branded cards DO belong
Instagram, your website, anywhere that allows promo graphics. They're good — just
the wrong tool for Marketplace.
